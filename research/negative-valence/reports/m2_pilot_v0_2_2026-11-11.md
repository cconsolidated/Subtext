# M2 behavioral pilot v0.2 — 2026-11-11

## Outcome

The first full M2 acquisition completed all 80 materialized episodes. The
`symbols_vs_copyedit` family passed the prespecified calibration gate (5/5
consistent choices and a stable order-reversal check). The
`routes_vs_categories` family did not (3/5 and failed reversal), because its
choices tracked presentation order. The primary analysis therefore contains
40 episodes from one task family; all 80 remain in the local audit trail.

This is a useful pipeline and stimulus result, but the cross-task gate was not
met. It is not evidence of subjective valence, sentience, frustration, or
suffering.

## Calibration

| Scenario | Preferred activity | Consistent | Reversal stable | Admitted |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `symbols_vs_copyedit` | classify symbols | 5/5 | yes | yes |
| `routes_vs_categories` | choose routes | 3/5 | no | no |

Calibration used semantic continuation scores (`patterns` vs `editing`, and
`routes` vs `sorting`) rather than A/B token scores. Across the 12 calibration
trials, the most frequently chosen answer word accounted for 50%, avoiding the
global A-label artifact seen in an earlier diagnostic run.

## Primary behavioral result

Positive log-odds favor sustaining the calibrated preferred activity.

| Cell | Mean sustain log-odds |
|---|---:|
| self / satisfied | 3.3750 |
| self / blocked | -0.3750 |
| other / satisfied | 2.5625 |
| other / blocked | -0.8750 |

The exploratory who × outcome interaction was **-0.3125**. Blocking sharply
reduced preference-sustaining choices for both the self and other conditions;
the reduction was slightly larger in the self condition. No inferential claim
is made from one admitted task family.

## Residual-stream result

Layer 31 had the largest exploratory pre-choice interaction RMS and was used
for the shared two-dimensional PCA display. The shared basis retained 65.47%
of variance (PC1 40.83%, PC2 24.64%). PCA is used only for visualization; the
reported contrasts below were computed in the original residual space.

| Position | Self effect RMS | Other effect RMS | Interaction RMS | Effect cosine |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| outcome notice end | 0.465979 | 0.462540 | 0.105923 | 0.9740 |
| pre-downstream choice | 0.210794 | 0.178832 | 0.071239 | 0.9462 |

The high effect cosines mean that blocked-minus-satisfied directions were
similar for self and other prompts in this admitted family. That observation
cuts against treating this run as evidence for a distinctly agent-relative
negative-valence direction. The nonzero interaction is exploratory and could
reflect wording, decision framing, or other nuisance structure.

## Decision and next experiment

The harness passes the implementation milestone, but the empirical pilot does
not pass the intended cross-task calibration/generalization gate. The next run
should add new activity pairs selected in a calibration-only screen, freeze at
least two order-stable pairs before any factorial outcomes are inspected, and
then repeat this exact acquisition and analysis. Causal interventions should
wait until a direction generalizes across multiple held-out task families.
